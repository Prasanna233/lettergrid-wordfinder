    Words

    Points: only cutting the opponent's letters (✂) scores — +1 for you, −1 for them per letter. Capturing free (empty) cells scores nothing. The first to 20 cells wins — reaching the opponent's base doesn't. Words that take you to 20 come first (🏁 WIN), then the ones that gain you the most while cutting the most of theirs. N/20 = your cells after the word. “opp needs N” = cells the opponent still needs to reach 20 after your word. For the top 40 words the app also finds their best reply: ⚠ red = they can reach 20 next move, ✓ = they can't.
